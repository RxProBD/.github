# RxProBD

**Advanced healthcare platform for doctors in Bangladesh.**

RxProBD is e-prescription and chamber management software for private doctors. Write and print prescriptions, keep patient records, and run your chambers from one account.

- **Prescriptions on one page.** Drug search from a verified catalogue with an allergy check, dosing in Bangla, templates and snippets, and printing on your own pad or letterhead.
- **Standard coding.** WHO ICD-11 for diagnoses and LOINC for lab orders, with free text when the standard has no match.
- **Copies for patients.** Each finalized prescription is emailed to the patient as a PDF. Paid plans also send an SMS link and automatic follow-up reminders.
- **Several chambers, one login.** Queues and daily serial numbers for each chamber, with accounts for your assistants and front-desk attendants.
- **Records beyond the prescription.** Medical certificates with a QR check, pregnancy records and expected deliveries, surgical records, and built-in clinical calculators.
- **Verified doctors.** Every doctor's BM&DC registration is verified. Doctors on paid plans can choose to appear in our public directory.
- **7-day free trial**, no payment needed.
